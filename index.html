Today's Top Picks

Jujutsu Kaisen is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Gege Akutami. It has been serialized in Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump magazine since March 2018, with its chapters collected in 24 tankōbon volumes as of July 2023. The story follows high school student Yūji Itadori as he joins a secret organization of Jujutsu Sorcerers to eliminate a powerful Curse named Ryomen Sukuna, of whom Yūji becomes the host.

Naruto is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Masashi Kishimoto. It follows the story of Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who seeks to gain recognition from his peers and dreams of becoming the Hokage, the leader of his village.

Death Note is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Tsugumi Ohba and Takeshi Obata. It follows the story of Light Yagami, a high school student who discovers a supernatural notebook that can kill anyone whose name is written in it.

Solo Leveling is a South Korean web novel written by Chugong. It follows the story of Sung Jin-Woo, a weak hunter who gains the ability to level up and become stronger after a mysterious event in a dungeon.
