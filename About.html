About Anime Haven

Anime Haven is a site made by me and my colleague Sophia. The site was made with the intention of centralizing a multitude of services provided to the anime community on various other sites, services such as Marketplaces for anime merch and related goods, fandoms, news and anime reviews. Our main goal is to provide a one-stop-shop for all things anime, and to create a community where fans can connect and share their love for anime.
